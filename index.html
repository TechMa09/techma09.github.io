    • About Me!

    I am a seasoned Enterprise, Data and Solution Architect recetly spearheaded teams as a technology advisor passionate about helping organizations understand their data and AI to solve real-world problems. Prototyped and presented custom GPT-based tools to C-suite leadership for evaluating vendor landscapes, while mentoring executives on data literacy and how AI can be applied to drive organizational improvement. I focus on helping organizations streamline operations, modernize legacy systems, and work more efficiently. For banking applications, I apply the BIAN framework to a certain extent, using it alongside TOGAF building blocks when designing systems and defining architectural components. When supporting platforms, I follow ITIL (Information Technology Infrastructure Library) framework.

    Previously

    I've been fortunate to witness the evolution of enterprise technology firsthand , from the mainframe era of IBM 4331, IBM 9375 and IBM 390 with COBOL and CICS, navigating green-screen 3270 terminals, through the rise of distributed thick-client architectures, and to SOAP APIs built on Java Spring, with application servers on IBM ESB and web servers on Apache. After 2000, I transitioned into building REST APIs. Later moved from traditional data center server and to cloud-native architectures with Kubernetes orchestrated Docker containers.

    I have worked for quite a number of financial institutions and across various lines of business (LOBs) including Retail, Lending, Risk Management, Data Management, Finance, asset management, global payments and automated processes to make Wall Street trading desks more efficient. Prior to that, I was part of a three-person team that relocated from Australia to join a leapfrog project at one of Chicago's largest banks, refactoring a thick-client call center application into a thin request-response server running on Apache and Tomcat, on-premise. During that time I used BoaP from BIAN (Banking Industry Architecture Network) to identify application boundaries. Earlier in my career in Sydney, I worked on Australia's first bank payment system, which was built on US-developed software, requiring me to troubleshoot issues on US business hours.

    So What?

    My advice to anyone reading this is simple: follow your passion, trust the process, and don't focus solely on outcomes. In the end, you'll get there anyway. I regularly write content from my current projects and past experiences. Thank you for visiting and come again to read new content!
